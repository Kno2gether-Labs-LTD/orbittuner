# Contributing to OrbitTuner

Thank you for wanting to help!

**Pull requests aren't being accepted yet.** OrbitTuner is still being set up, and there isn't enough maintainer time to review contributions properly. Contributions will open soon; this page will say so when they do.

## What helps right now
- **Bug reports.** Open an issue with the *Feedback* template: your phone and Android version, the instrument, what you did and what you saw.
- **Tunings and instruments.** Missing a tuning you use? Tell us the instrument and the notes, lowest to highest.
- **Ideas.** Tell us what would make OrbitTuner your everyday practice app.

Please don't post song lyrics or chord sheets that you don't have the rights to share in a public issue.

## Security problems
Please don't open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
